Singapore, 12 th August, 2025 - Naluri Pte Ltd , (“Naluri”), Southeast Asia’s leading digital employee health and wellbeing provider, today announced the successful completion of a USD 5 million Series-B equity fundraising round. This latest round was led by TELUS Global Ventures - through the Pollinator Fund for Good - the strategic impact investment arm of world-leading communications technology company, TELUS, alongside existing investors Sumitomo Corporation Equity Asia, M Venture Partners, and others.
