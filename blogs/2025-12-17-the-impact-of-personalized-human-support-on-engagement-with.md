Digital healthcare has grown in popularity in recent years as a scalable solution to address increasing rates of mental illness among employees. Still, its clinical potential is limited by low engagement and adherence, particularly in open-access interventions. Personalised guidance, structuring and tailoring an intervention to the user to increase accountability and social support, is one way to increase engagement with digital health programs.
